STATUS (same for projects and blogs, handled by _plugins/status.rb):
  done    visible everywhere, the default when status is left out
  wip     visible, with a work in progress banner and an "In progress" card badge
  draft   only in local builds (jekyll serve), with a draft banner, left out of the live site
  hidden  never built, in any environment
