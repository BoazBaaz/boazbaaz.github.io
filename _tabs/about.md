## About Me

My name is Boas-Bas van der Veen. I'm a fourth-year student at Breda University of Applied Sciences, studying Creative Media and Game Technologies with a specialization in engine and tools programming.

I've been playing games since I was 7 years old, starting with **Pokémon HeartGold** and **The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks** on the Nintendo DS. My mom is a big Nintendo fan, so I mostly grew up with Nintendo games.

When I got older, I bought my first PC and started playing games like **Minecraft** and **Terraria**. I loved the freedom, exploration and discovery those games gave me. During the pandemic in 2020 I started playing **Final Fantasy XIV Online** and fell in love with its world, which led me to try all kinds of MMOs, from **World of Warcraft** to **Guild Wars 2**. Since then I've played and explored a ton of games, from **Elden Ring** and **Valheim** to **Warframe**.

In recent years I've also been working on being healthier. I started exercising and paying more attention to what I eat, which helped me lose a lot of weight and reach a healthy range for the first time in my adult life. I've kept those habits going, with walks and exercise three to five times a week.

## Experience & Education

I completed a Game Developer diploma at **Grafisch Lyceum Utrecht**. It was a four-year program where I learned to design and program games in **C#** using the **Unity** engine, and made a lot of Unity projects along the way.

For my fourth-year internship there, I spent a year at the **Koninklijke Landmacht** _(Royal Netherlands Army)_, contributing to **Virtual Battle Space** _(VBS)_ training scenarios. I applied what I had learned to build realistic military simulations. That experience taught me the value of collaboration and of building robust technical solutions under real-world constraints.

I'm now pursuing a Bachelor's in Creative Media and Game Technologies at **Breda University of Applied Sciences**, specializing in **Engine & Tools Programming**. Here I've mostly worked in custom **C++** engines and **Unreal Engine**. I've built game engines from scratch, developed **Unreal Engine** plugins and editor tools, and worked on **C++** engine architecture, editor tooling and cross-platform build systems using **CMake**.
