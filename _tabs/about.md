## About Me

My name is Boas-Bas van der Veen, I am currently a fourth-year student at Breda University of Applied Sciences pursuing Creative Media and Game Technologies with a specialization in engine and tools programming. <br>

I have been playing games ever since I was 7 years old, starting with **Pokémon HeartGold** and **The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks** on the Nintendo DS. 
Due to my mom being a big Nintendo fan I grew up with mostly Nintendo games as a kid. <br>
Later once I was older I bought my first PC and started playing games like **Minecraft** and **Terraria**, I loved the freedom, exploration and discovery those games provided. <br> 
Then during the global pandemic in 2020 I started playing **Final Fantasy XIV Online** and fell in love with what world, later playing and trying all kinds of MMOs from **World of Warcraft** to **Guild Wars 2**. <br>
Now I have a ton of games I have enjoyed and explored, from games like **Elden Ring** and **Valheim** to **Warframe**. 

Then in recent year I have also been trying to be more healthy, I started exercising and paying more attention to what I ate.
This caused me to lose a lot of weight and be in the standard ranges for the first time in my adult life. 
I have continued to keep healthy habits like taking walks and exercising three to five times a week.


## Experience & Education

At this time I have finished a Game Developer diploma at **Graphies Lyceum Utrecht**, this was a four-year course where I learned both how to design games and program games in **C#** using the **Unity** engine.

I am currently pursuing a Batchelor in Creative Media and Game Technologies at **Breda University of Applied Sciences**, specializing in **Engine & Tools Programming**. During my years here I have mostly worked in custom **C++** engine and **Unreal Engine**. <br>

I've built custom game engines from scratch, developed **Unreal Engine** plugins and editor tools, and created numerous Unity projects throughout my academic career. My technical expertise spans **C++** engine architecture, editor tooling, and cross-platform build systems using **CMake**.

During my time at **Graphies Lyceum Utrecht** as my year four internship I worked at **Koninklijke Landmacht** _(Dutch Royal Army)_ for a year, I contributed to **Virtual Battle Space** _(VBS)_ training scenarios, applying the skills I had learned to develop realistic military simulations. This experience taught me the importance of collaboration and the importance of developing robust technical solutions under real-world constraints.
